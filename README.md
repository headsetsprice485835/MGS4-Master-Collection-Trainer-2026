## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer provides optional single-player enhancements that modify real gameplay systems such as health, Psyche, ammunition, suppressor durability, alert status, Drebin Points, and camouflage. It is designed to make replaying missions, testing weapons, and exploring the campaign more convenient without changing the core stealth-action structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay the campaign with fewer restrictions, test weapons and equipment, practice stealth routes, explore missions freely, or reduce grinding during repeated playthroughs.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Solid Snake's life gauge from reaching zero during gameplay.
- **Infinite Psyche** — Keeps Snake's Psyche at maximum to reduce the effects of battlefield stress and mental fatigue.
- **No Alert** — Prevents normal enemy alert escalation and keeps the mission in a low-threat state.
- **Stealth Camouflage** — Activates an enhanced stealth effect inspired by the game's OctoCamo and stealth equipment systems.
- **Freeze Timer** — Stops active mission and challenge timers from advancing.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Infinite Ammo** — Prevents ammunition reserves from decreasing for equipped firearms and compatible weapons.
- **No Reload** — Stops ammunition in the current magazine from being consumed, allowing continuous fire without standard reloads.
- **Unlimited Suppressor Durability** — Prevents equipped firearm suppressors from wearing out during use.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Max Drebin Points** — Sets Snake's Drebin Points to a high value for purchasing weapons, ammunition, upgrades, and equipment from Drebin's mobile shop.
- **Infinite Item Usage** — Prevents selected consumable items and support equipment from being depleted.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through active battlefields using normal movement, crouching, crawling, cover, and contextual actions. OctoCamo adapts Snake's appearance to nearby surfaces, while the trainer's stealth and alert options can be used to practice routes without repeated detection. Keep the trainer disabled during scripted sequences if a mission does not respond correctly.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Snake's weapon and item menus normally. Drebin Points can be increased to purchase firearms, ammunition, suppressors, weapon upgrades, and equipment. Infinite ammunition and item usage affect resource consumption but do not replace the normal weapon-selection and equipment-management systems.

### World & Environment

The campaign moves through several war zones where opposing factions can influence the battlefield. Enemies may fight one another, civilians can be present, and mission conditions can change as combat develops. The trainer is intended for controlled exploration of these areas and should be toggled off when preserving the game's normal alert, score, and progression behavior.

### Combat & Weapons

MGS4 supports third-person shooting, first-person aiming, close-quarters combat, suppressed weapons, explosives, and weapon customization. Use infinite health, Psyche, ammunition, or suppressor durability to test different combat approaches. The No Alert option is best suited to stealth practice, while unlimited resources are useful for experimenting with weapons and enemy encounters.
